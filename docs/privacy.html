Privacy Policy

Send Your Strength · Version 2026-11-17

Draft for legal review. This policy has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

Send Your Strength is operated by Artificial Intelligence Consulting LLC. It is used by high school athletes, so we collect as little as we can and keep it only as long as the fundraiser needs it.

We never see or store card numbers or bank details. Payments are made through the team's own payment provider. To limit abuse, we keep a scrambled (hashed) form of a visitor's network address for a short time; we never store the address itself.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent any information.
  • We don't use it for advertising, and the site has no ad trackers.
  • We don't use it to train AI models.
  • We don't list athletes publicly or let search engines index the site.

How long we keep it

Athlete and sponsor information is deleted 90 days after the team's season ends, unless the team renews for another season. Records of terms acceptance are kept for two years.

Your choices

  • An athlete (or a parent) can unpublish the athlete's page at any time from the athlete's sign-in.
  • Anyone can ask us to correct or delete their information. Email hello@sendyourstrength.com. A parent can ask on behalf of an athlete.

Students

The site is meant for high school athletes. An athlete's page can't be published without a parent's or guardian's approval. If you believe information about a student was added without proper consent, contact us and we'll remove it.

Security

Browsers can only read information; every change goes through server code that checks who is asking. Sign-in uses one-time email links, and organizer access is re-checked at every sign-in. If we learn of a breach affecting a team's information, we'll notify the team within 72 hours.
